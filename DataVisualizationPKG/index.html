Student Loan Complaints Persist After Navient Transition

By Sol Garcia-Terrazas

Federal student loan servicing shifted in late 2021 when Aidvantage, a Maximus division, took over millions of accounts from Navient.

While loan terms and interest rates remained the same, the transition did little to ease borrower frustration. Consumer Financial Protection Bureau data shows that complaints remained elevated, suggesting servicer changes did not resolve manegment issues.

The reports primarily cite repayment difficulties and servicer interctions. These finding align with borrower experiences reported by the Student Loan Planner, including long wait times, unclear pyment information and inconsistent support.

The data suggests the central challenge is navigating repayment, a problem that persists despite the move from Navient to Aidvantage.
